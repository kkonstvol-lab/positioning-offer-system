# Agency Positioning Example

## Positioning options
### Option A
Full-service growth partner for expert brands.
- Too broad, low distinctiveness.

### Option B
Content systems agency for expert-led businesses.
- Better, but still incomplete.

### Option C
Agency that converts expertise into repeatable content and demand-generation systems.
- Clear result
- Stronger mechanism
- Better differentiation from generic content agencies

## Chosen direction
Option C, because it is clearer, more outcome-linked, and easier to translate into landing structure.
