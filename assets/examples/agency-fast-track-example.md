# Agency Fast Track Example

## Input snapshot
- Agency helps experts and founders package expertise into content and AI-assisted funnels.
- Main pain: the offer sounds too broad and generic.
- Goal: build a landing that sells a sharper agency offer.

## Context summary
- Buyer: expert-led business owner who wants content to drive demand, not just output.
- Main problem: current agencies sell content production, not a business outcome.
- Current weakness: no sharp mechanism, no clear commercial promise.

## Positioning
Agency that turns expert knowledge into a content system tied to conversion assets, not random content production.

## UTP
We help expert-led businesses turn expertise into a content-and-conversion system through strategy, production, and AI-assisted workflows — without building an expensive in-house content machine.

## Offer
- positioning cleanup
- content system design
- funnel-linked content production
- AI workflow integration
- landing/message refinement

## Landing skeleton
### 1. Hero
Turn expertise into a content system that drives demand, not just output.

### 2. Problem
Why generic content agencies create activity without conversion logic.

### 3. Mechanism
How the agency turns raw expertise into structured content plus conversion assets.

### 4. Offer
What is included, for whom, and what business outcome it supports.

### 5. Proof
Cases, strategic artifacts, process logic, before/after packaging examples.

### 6. CTA
Book a strategy call / request packaging audit.
