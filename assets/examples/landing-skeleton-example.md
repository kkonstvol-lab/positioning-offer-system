# Landing Skeleton Example

## 1. Hero
For expert-led businesses that need content to drive demand, not just fill a feed.

## 2. Problem
Why generic agencies produce activity without strategic leverage.

## 3. Mechanism
How the agency turns raw expertise into a structured content and conversion engine.

What is delivered, in what format, for whom, and what outcome it is designed to support.

## 5. Proof
Cases, examples, process logic, strategic artifacts.

## 6. Objections
Why this is not another vague content retainer.

## 7. CTA
Book a strategy call / request packaging audit / discuss landing rebuild.
