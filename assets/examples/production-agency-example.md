White space: almost nobody leads with the content-after-event angle or names specific platforms/formats (YouTube podcasts for large corporations).

## Positioning statement
> We help large companies create corporate podcasts, events, and video content through a full production cycle — so the content keeps working after the event ends.

## UTP
Full-cycle corporate production: from brief to publishing and distribution — without hiring an in-house team and without losing quality at any stage.

## Offer
**Deliverables:**
- Concept and script development
- Full production (shooting, streaming, editing)
- Publishing and post-production
- Post-event content (clips, highlights, podcast episodes)

**Format:** project-based or retainer
**Timeline:** from 2 weeks (single event) to 6-month retainer
**For:** PR/communications managers at companies with 500+ employees
**Not for:** individual entrepreneurs, startups without a dedicated PR budget
**CTA:** "Discuss your project" → form → reply within 24h

## Landing skeleton

### Hero
Headline: We produce podcasts and events that keep working after the event ends
Subline: Full cycle from concept to publishing — for large companies with reputation standards
CTA: Discuss your project

### Problem
- The event is over — content disappeared. No materials for further use
- The vendor delivers the picture but doesn't think about what to do with it next
- Approvals drag on, deadlines burn, accountability is blurred

### Mechanism
1. Brief — goals, format, audience, expected result
2. Concept — narrative framework, content logic
3. Preparation — team, timeline, venue or studio
4. Production — shooting, streaming, or live event
5. Post-production — editing, publishing, platform cuts

### Offer block
- Corporate events (conferences, forums, retreats, industry awards)
- Online streams on YouTube, VK, and other platforms
- Full-cycle podcasts and video content
- Single point of contact throughout the project

### Proof
1. Industrial holding — safety podcast: 5 episodes, key episode hit 10K views in 8 hours
2. Same holding — youth employer brand podcast: 11 episodes with C-level executives
3. Corporate HR marathon: 3 days × 8h live stream on multiple platforms
4. Industry award that grew into a national-level event

### Objections
- "You don't know our industry" → show a relevant case, offer a pilot
- "Too expensive" → upfront payment for first phase only, then by milestone
- "We had bad vendor experiences" → one manager = one point of accountability
- "We need it fast" → standard launch from 2 weeks

### CTA
Primary: Discuss your project (form: name, company, task)
Secondary: View case studies (anchor to proof section)
After form: "We'll reply within 24 hours and propose a format for your task."
