# UTP and offer framework

Use after positioning is chosen.

## UTP formula
**Result + audience + method + timeframe + differentiator**

Example: «Помогаем производственным компаниям снизить количество инцидентов через ежеквартальный внутренний подкаст с топами — без найма редакции и постоянного согласования.»

Simple English formula:
> We help [specific client] get [valuable result] through [distinctive approach], without [common frustration / weaker alternative].

## Build the offer
Turn positioning into a concrete commercial offer:
- what exactly is delivered (deliverables)
- in what format (online, on-site, retainer, project)
- in what scope (how many, how often)
- in what timeline (start → finish)
- with what proof or confidence signal (case, guarantee, pilot)
- with what CTA or next step (call, audit, pilot proposal)

## Offer refinement checklist
- Is the promise strong enough to matter to a busy decision-maker?
- Is it specific enough to understand in 10 seconds?
- Is it believable without a sales call?
- Is the mechanism visible or still vague?
- Does the offer remove friction or create new friction?
- Can it be rejected? (if anyone can say yes, the offer is too broad)

## Output
- One-line UTP (≤30 words)
- Expanded offer statement (2-3 sentences)
- 3-5 offer bullets (what's included)
- 3-5 objection-handling hooks
- CTA direction (what is the exact next step)
