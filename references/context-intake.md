# Context intake

Goal: collect the minimum viable input to build sharp positioning and offer. No questionnaire overload — gather what moves the needle.

## Block 1 — Business
- What exactly is sold today? (service name, format, scope)
- What result does the client get? (concrete, measurable)
- How long does the engagement last?
- What is the average deal size / price range?
- Who is NOT a fit? (define the anti-customer)

## Block 2 — Target audience
- Which niches / industries do you work with?
- What is the size of the client business? (team, revenue)
- Who makes the buying decision? (title, role)
- What channels bring clients today?

## Block 3 — Client situation
- What have clients tried before coming to you?
- Where have they been burned by other vendors?
- What do they complain about most in the market?
- Why haven't they solved this problem yet?

## Block 4 — Motivation and barriers
- What does the client really want? (business outcome, not feature)
- What are they afraid of?
- What objections do you hear most often?
- Why do deals stall or fall through?

## If a document exists
If the user provides a website URL, PDF, or presentation: read it first.
Extract: current positioning claims, services listed, case studies, stated differentiators, pricing signals, team info.
Note what sounds weak, generic, or provably false.

## Output of this step
- Short business context snapshot (5-7 lines)
- List of open strategic gaps
- Voice-of-customer phrases if any provided
- Recommended mode: Fast Track / Deep Positioning / Landing Copy Mode
