# Anti-patterns

Common failures that produce weak positioning and landing pages. Recognize and fix on contact.

## Intake anti-patterns
- Asking for positioning without business context first
- Accepting "we help businesses grow" as a description of what is sold
- Not asking about anti-customers (who you don't serve)
- Not asking what clients tried before and where they were burned
- Treating "target audience" as a sector, not a person with a job title and a budget

## Positioning anti-patterns
- Positioning that applies to any agency ("professional team", "individual approach")
- Leading with values instead of outcomes ("we care about your success")
- Using the company name as the positioning ("we are Acme, and we do marketing")
- Competing on execution quality instead of mechanism or scope
- Trying to address everyone → positioning for no one
- Compromise positioning that tries to hold three incompatible angles

## UTP anti-patterns
- "Quality, speed, price" — pick two (everyone says this)
- "We go deep" without explaining what depth means
- Result described as "growth" without a number or mechanism
- Differentiator that is actually a table stake ("we meet deadlines")
- UTP that the client cannot verify before buying

## Offer anti-patterns
- Listing services instead of deliverables
- No timeline or scope — sounds infinite and therefore expensive
- Price anchor missing when it would reduce friction
- CTA leads to a contact form with no stated next step
- Multiple CTAs competing for attention

## Copy anti-patterns
- Hero headline starting with "We are..."
- Subline repeating the headline in different words
- Proof section with logos only, no outcomes
- FAQ that answers questions nobody asks
- Objection section that sounds defensive instead of confident

## Process anti-patterns
- Skipping competitive research → positioning sounds generic
- Skipping critique step → weak strategy reaches the landing
- No iteration after critique → first draft becomes final
- Saving output without a named file → findings lost
