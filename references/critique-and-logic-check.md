# Critique and logic check

Use this step to kill weak strategy before it reaches the landing.
Run TWO passes: self-critique, then logic check.

## Pass 1 — Self-critique (enter critic mode)
Act as a skeptical marketing critic. Find every weak point:

- Generic claims that could fit 100 other agencies
- No clear audience (who exactly is this for?)
- Weak or abstract result (what does the client actually get?)
- No distinctive mechanism (how is this different from anyone else?)
- Too much branding language, too little commercial clarity
- Claims stronger than available proof
- Offer and positioning aimed at different buyers
- Sounds pretty but does not sell

Ruthless prompts:
- Which sentence could a competitor copy-paste unchanged?
- What is still vague?
- Where is the proof gap?
- If a skeptical CFO sees this, what will they distrust first?
- What is the real commercial hook — is it visible in the first 5 seconds?

## Pass 2 — Logic check
Check marketing logic:
- Is it clear what is being sold?
- Is the concrete result visible?
- Is it clear who this is for — and who it is NOT for?
- Is there genuine differentiation or a label on a commodity?
- Can the promise be substantiated?
- Is there pafos and cliché instead of specifics?
- Does the text build trust with a business owner?

## Scoring rubric (0–100)
Score the current positioning/UTP across 5 dimensions (0–20 each):

| Dimension | 0–20 |
|---|---|
| Clarity — understood in 5 sec without context | /20 |
| Specificity — concrete audience + result + mechanism | /20 |
| Distinctiveness — can't be said by 10 competitors | /20 |
| Believability — proof exists or is clearly stated as a bet | /20 |
| Commercial pull — makes the right buyer want to know more | /20 |

**Gate:** if total score < 70 → go back to Step 2 (positioning) or Step 3 (UTP), rewrite, re-score.
Maximum 2 loops before outputting best available version with explicit caveats.

## Output
- Critique list (specific problems, not categories)
- Pass 2 logic findings
- Score (0–100) with per-dimension breakdown
- Corrected positioning/offer version
- What must be proved on the landing (mandatory proof blocks)
