# Landing translation

Goal: translate positioning and offer into a practical, conversion-ready landing structure.

## Core landing flow (7 sections)

### 1. Hero
**Job:** communicate who it's for + core result + sharp angle in one screen.
- Headline: ≤12 words, outcome-first or problem-first (never "we are...")
- Subline: ≤25 words, adds the mechanism or proof signal
- CTA button: action verb + what happens next ("Обсудить проект", "Получить разбор")
- Visual: supports the claim, not decorates it

### 2. Problem framing
**Job:** make the reader feel understood before presenting the solution.
- Name the expensive / frustrating status quo
- Name why current alternatives fail (freelancer, in-house, generic agency)
- 3-4 bullets or short paragraphs, written in client language

### 3. Mechanism
**Job:** explain how this agency works differently — make the claim believable.
- Name the process, method, or approach
- Show the 3-5 step workflow (named stages, not generic "discovery → delivery")
- Connect mechanism to result: "Because of X, you get Y"

### 4. Offer block
**Job:** make the commercial offer concrete and scannable.
- What is included (deliverables)
- Format and scope
- Timeline or cadence
- Who it is for / who it is NOT for
- Price anchor or "starting from" if available

### 5. Proof
**Job:** substantiate the claims with evidence.
Priority order (strongest first):
1. Named case studies with concrete numbers
2. Client logos (recognizable brands)
3. Process artifacts (actual deliverables, frameworks, screenshots)
4. Testimonials with specific outcomes
5. Team credentials

### 6. Objections
**Job:** address the 3-5 most common reasons a qualified buyer hesitates.
Typical objections: cost, trust ("can you handle our scale?"), fit ("is this for us?"), speed, "we tried this before"
Format: FAQ, accordion, or inline callouts

### 7. CTA
**Job:** give one clear next step that reduces friction.
- One primary CTA (not three)
- State what happens after clicking ("We'll reply within 24h")
- Optional: secondary CTA for not-ready-yet visitors (case study, audit, checklist)

## Copy quality rules
- No sentence over 20 words
- No unverifiable adjectives ("innovative", "cutting-edge", "world-class", "best-in-class")
- Every claim: either proven (link to case) or framed as a bet
- Write for a skeptical, time-poor decision-maker
- Mobile-first: headline must work at 320px width

## Output
- Section list with one-line job for each
- Hero draft (headline + subline + CTA)
- Offer block draft
- Proof block priority list
- CTA direction and form/next-step logic
- Full landing skeleton in MD format
